Common questions

Can Civic Compass tell me if I am registered?

No. We do not check voter records and we never claim you are registered or eligible. Your state election office runs the official lookup. We link to it rather than repeating what it says.

Why does a contest say you have no data?

Election data is published by thousands of separate offices in different formats and on different schedules. When we cannot confirm something from a responsible authority, we say so rather than guessing. An empty space would read as “there is nothing here,” which is a different and more damaging claim.

Why do two dates sometimes appear for the same deadline?

Occasionally two election authorities publish different information. Rather than picking one, we show both and send you to the election office. We display the earlier date in checklists, because acting earlier is the safer error, but we tell you we are doing it.

Does Civic Compass recommend candidates?

No, and it is built so that it cannot. There is no ranking, scoring, or matching feature, no advertising, and no paid placement. Entries within a contest use one shared template, so they cannot be given different visual weight.

What do you do with my address?

We use it to work out which jurisdictions you are in, then discard it. It is never written to a database, never placed in a web address, and never shared. We do not record or infer anything about your political preferences.
