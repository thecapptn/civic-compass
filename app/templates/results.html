What happens after election night

Canvass

After polls close, election officials check and reconcile the count: ballots that arrived late but on time, ballots that needed review, and any discrepancies between the number of ballots and the number of voters. This process is called the canvass, and it takes days to weeks depending on the jurisdiction.

Certification

When the canvass is complete, the responsible authority certifies the result. Certification is what makes a result official. Before that point, any number you see — including on this page — is a report of a count in progress.

Recounts

Some jurisdictions recount automatically when a margin is very close; in others, a recount can be requested. A recount can change a reported result.

The U.S. Election Assistance Commission explains this process at eac.gov. Only election officials publish official results.
